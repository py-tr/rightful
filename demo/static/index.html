How it works, and what this demo does not claim

The agent writes scripts that run in a disposable sandbox holding no credential. Every account change it tries is refused by the gate and turned into a request on the owner's phone that names the action and the account. Only the owner's yes to that exact request lets it through, once. The rightmost column of the receipt is read from the identity server's own log with a view-only account, not from our software. Simulated: the phone, the people, the inbox (we only show that an email arrived). The cooling-off is 20 seconds here (production: 60 seconds, 10 minutes for privileged accounts).

Not claimed: that people always read a phone request carefully (we found no study showing it), that a sandbox is an absolute boundary, or that the model is safe. The claim is narrower: without the owner's approval of the exact request, the identity server records no change.
